# Family stays

Document ID: family-policy | Revision: 1 | Status: published

## Occupancy and bedding

Every adult and child counts towards the villa's guest capacity; this demo has no infant occupancy exemption. Forest Suite accommodates two guests in one king bed. Garden Villa accommodates four guests across two bedrooms: one king bed and two single beds. No extra beds are available.

A cot for a child under two may be requested without charge, subject to confirmation, but does not increase capacity. Ask the children's ages if bedding or activities depend on age; do not request names or birth dates for a recommendation.

## Facilities and activities

Private pools are unfenced. No lifeguard is provided. Adults must supervise children; do not describe a villa as childproof. There is no kids' club or babysitting service. Guided village walks accept children aged six and over with an accompanying adult. Yoga and spa services are for guests aged 16 and over.

## Meals

Children registered within the villa capacity have breakfast included when their accommodation rate includes it. Other meals are charged from the restaurant menu, which is not published here. Do not invent children's menu prices or discounts.
