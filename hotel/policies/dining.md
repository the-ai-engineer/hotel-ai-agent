# Breakfast and dining

Document ID: dining-policy | Revision: 2 | Status: published

All service charges are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR), including tax. This is fictional demo information.

## Breakfast

Breakfast is included in the demo flexible accommodation rate for registered guests, including children within the villa capacity. Other rates must be checked against their terms. Restaurant breakfast runs daily from 07:00 to 10:30.

Options include tropical fruit, porridge, pastries and eggs cooked to order. Vegan breakfast options include fruit, coconut porridge and vegetable rice; plant-based milk is available. Terrace breakfast has an additional delivery charge of IDR 150,000 per villa per morning, regardless of party size. Request it by 18:00 the previous day; the delivery time requires staff confirmation.

## Allergies

Tell staff about allergies before arrival. Staff can discuss suitable meals, but the kitchen handles nuts, dairy and gluten and cannot guarantee an allergen-free meal or environment. The concierge cannot confirm a safe meal or record that staff have received an allergy request. Do not ask for medical history.

## Restaurant and private dining

Lunch is served 12:00–15:00 and dinner 18:00–22:00. A three-course private terrace dinner costs IDR 1,800,000 for two adults, excluding drinks. Request it at least 48 hours in advance. Vegetarian and vegan versions can be requested. Dietary arrangements, date and time require staff confirmation; the concierge cannot reserve a table or dinner.
