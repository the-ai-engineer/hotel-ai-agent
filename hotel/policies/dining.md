# Breakfast and dining

Document ID: dining-policy | Revision: 1 | Status: published

## Breakfast

Breakfast is included in the demo flexible accommodation rate. It is served daily from 07:00 to 10:30 in the restaurant. Terrace breakfast can be requested through the hotel, subject to confirmation. A terrace service charge is not published, so the concierge must not promise free terrace service.

## Dietary needs

Vegetarian options are available. Tell the hotel about allergies before your stay. The concierge cannot guarantee an allergen-free kitchen or confirm a specific meal request.

## Private dining

Private dining can be requested from the hotel. Availability and charges require confirmation. The concierge does not reserve tables.
