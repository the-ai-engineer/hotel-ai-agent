# Demo reservations and requests

Document ID: booking-policy | Revision: 1 | Status: published

## Reserve a villa

The concierge checks exact dates and total guests, including children and infants. The matching villa card includes Reserve this villa. This opens the booking page with those details filled in. The guest reviews the stay and clicks Confirm reservation. The backend rechecks every night before creating a confirmed demo reservation and returning its booking reference. Availability results do not hold a room. No payment is taken and no accommodation price is quoted. Sanctuary Hotel is fictional; this is not a real hotel reservation.

## Look up your booking

The concierge can look up a reservation belonging to this browser's guest session, by its reference or the latest booking. A reference alone is not access permission. The demo guest session lasts 24 hours. A booking from a different or expired session cannot be retrieved here. Returning-guest authentication is outside this demo.

## Note to the hotel

The concierge can prepare a note linked to an owned booking. The guest reviews it and clicks Send request. Only then is it recorded as Pending hotel review. This does not change checkout, confirm transfers or activities, guarantee dietary arrangements, or send an external email. Staff approval remains necessary for such arrangements. A draft expires after ten minutes or is replaced by a newer draft. Starting a new conversation invalidates unsubmitted drafts. Confirmed reservations are retained across conversation resets within the same guest session.
