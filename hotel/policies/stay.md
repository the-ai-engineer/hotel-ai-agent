# Arrival and departure

Document ID: stay-policy | Revision: 2 | Status: published

Sanctuary Hotel is a fictional luxury forest retreat in Bali. Property timezone: Asia/Makassar. All published service charges are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR), including tax.

## Check-in and early arrival

Check-in starts at 15:00. Checkout is by 11:00. Early room access requires staff confirmation and is not guaranteed, even if the villa is available for that night's stay.

Guests arriving early may store luggage with reception from 07:00, use the restaurant from 07:00 and the shared pool from 08:00. Changing rooms and showers are beside the shared pool. These facilities do not require early access to the villa. Reception is staffed 24 hours; guests arriving after 22:00 should notify the hotel beforehand.

## Airport transfers

A private airport transfer costs IDR 450,000 per car, each way, for up to four passengers. Children count as passengers. A child seat can be requested without an extra charge, subject to confirmation. Provide flight number, arrival date/time and passenger count at least 24 hours before arrival. The concierge cannot confirm a transfer reservation.

Allow roughly 90 minutes between the airport and hotel, depending on traffic. This is a planning estimate, not a live traffic report. For departure, the hotel recommends leaving at least four hours before an international flight; confirm timing with staff.

## Late checkout and departure facilities

Checkout until 14:00 costs IDR 500,000 per villa if approved by staff. Checkout after 14:00 requires a separate quote. Requests should be made by 18:00 the previous day. Availability search does not establish housekeeping readiness or approve late checkout.

After checkout, guests may store luggage until 22:00 and use the shared pool and its changing/shower facilities until 18:00. Villa access ends at the agreed checkout time. Restaurant dining is available during published opening hours and remains chargeable.

## Pets and access

Pets are not permitted, except assistance animals arranged with the hotel. Step-free access is not confirmed in published information. Seek staff confirmation before booking accessible accommodation; no contact destination is configured in this demo.
