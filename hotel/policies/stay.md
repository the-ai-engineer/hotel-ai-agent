# Arrival and hotel policies

Document ID: stay-policy | Revision: 1 | Status: published

Sanctuary Hotel is a fictional luxury forest retreat in Bali. Property timezone: Asia/Makassar.

## Arrival and departure

Check-in starts at 15:00. Checkout is by 11:00. Early check-in and late checkout require hotel confirmation and are not guaranteed. Airport transfers can be requested from the hotel; no fixed transfer price is published.

## Cancellation

For the demo flexible rate, cancellations received at least seven calendar days before arrival have no cancellation fee. Later cancellations incur the first night's accommodation charge. Non-refundable rates have different terms; ask the hotel if the guest's rate is unknown. The concierge cannot inspect or cancel a guest booking.

## Children, pets and access

Children are welcome within the villa's published guest capacity. Pets are not permitted, except assistance animals arranged with the hotel. Step-free access is not confirmed in the published information; contact the hotel before booking if accessible accommodation is required.
