# Guest services

Document ID: services-policy | Revision: 2 | Status: published

## Wi-Fi and pools

Wi-Fi is included in each villa and the restaurant. The private pool is for the villa's registered guests. The shared pool is open 08:00–20:00; departing guests may use it only until 18:00. Children need adult supervision at all pools. There is no lifeguard. Access passwords are not published in this public knowledge base.

## Spa

The spa opens daily from 09:00 to 20:00 for guests aged 16 and over. A 60-minute massage costs IDR 650,000 per person, including tax. Request appointments at least 24 hours ahead. Opening hours do not prove appointment availability. Staff must confirm appointments; the concierge cannot book a treatment.

## Staff confirmation

Reception is staffed 24 hours. Services needing confirmation remain requests, not reservations. No contact destination or staffed chat is configured in this demo. Explain that limitation; do not invent a phone number, email address, booking link or claim that a request was sent.
