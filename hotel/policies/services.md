# Guest services

Document ID: services-policy | Revision: 1 | Status: published

## Wi-Fi and pool

Wi-Fi is included in every villa. Each villa has a private pool. Children must be supervised at the pool.

## Activities and spa

Yoga, guided village walks and spa treatments can be requested from the hotel. No daily schedule or treatment prices are published. Do not invent tomorrow's activities or confirm a spa appointment.

## Human assistance

Requests needing confirmation must be handled by hotel staff. No contact destination is configured in this starting website. Explain that limitation until an approved contact destination is added. This demonstration has no staffed chat or working reservation channel. Do not invent a phone number, email address, live transfer or booking link.
